=== 4. Closing order and suppressed exceptions ===
-- (a) normal run, two resources: closing is in REVERSE order of opening
   open  A (opened first)
   open  B (opened second)
   body runs
   close B (opened second)
   close A (opened first)
-- (b) body throws AND close() throws: the BODY exception wins, the close exception is SUPPRESSED
   open  A
   open  B
   close B
   close A
   caught: exception from the body
   suppressed: close() of B failed
   suppressed: close() of A failed
-- (c) same situation with manual try/finally: the close exception REPLACES the body exception (original lost)
   open  A
   close A
   caught: close() of A failed   (suppressed: 0)
